Can AI facilitate learning better
while empowering educators?

Ms. Sonoma is a K–8 learning platform built to explore that question. The AI actively leads a structured session; the educator chooses, reviews, approves, and interprets the learning. Mastery—not screen time or mere completion—is the aim.

The learning question

Understanding should drive the session.

A learner needs more than content delivery. They need questions, practice, feedback, another attempt, and a clear record of what happened. Ms. Sonoma gives AI an active facilitation role inside a repeatable learning sequence while keeping authority with the educator.

A session organized around evidence of understanding.

The sequence moves from conversation and instruction into guided practice and assessment. Practice phases can include feedback, hints, and additional attempts; the final record helps the educator decide what comes next.

1

Discussion

Connects the learner to the lesson through guided conversation.

2

Teaching

Presents educator-approved vocabulary, ideas, and examples.

3

Comprehension

Checks what the learner understands and where support is needed.

4

Exercise

Uses conversational practice and immediate feedback.

5

Worksheet

Applies the lesson through structured questions.

6

Test

Separates independent assessment from guided practice.

7

Congratulations

Closes the experience and records the completed session.

Important: Completing the flow is not proof of durable mastery. The transcript, responses, scores, and facilitator notes are evidence for an educator to interpret, revisit, or build on.

The educator remains the author and authority.

AI facilitates the session. It does not decide the learner’s curriculum, certify mastery, or replace professional or parental judgment.

Bring or choose material

Review and approve

Assign and schedule

Choose who receives a lesson and when it enters their learning plan.

Interpret the evidence

Use session records and observations to decide whether to continue, reteach, or revise.

The hypothesis

Why might AI facilitate learning well?

AI can repeat without frustration, maintain a consistent sequence, ask follow-up questions, and provide feedback during practice. Those are structural capabilities worth testing. They are not, by themselves, evidence that AI produces better learning outcomes than a skilled human educator.

The method

Calm supports the work.

Calm is not the mission. It is a method: deliberate pacing, a predictable flow, a low-distraction interface, and a present adult who can observe and step in. The purpose is to create conditions where attention, practice, and understanding can develop.
